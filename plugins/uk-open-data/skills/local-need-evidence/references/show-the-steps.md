# Show the steps

For a person who asks to see the steps, to understand a result before using it, or how a figure was made. Take one stage in each reply and stop for the person at the end of it, with a question.

1. The question, the analysis that answers it and what the analysis takes.
2. What goes in, which door it goes through and what, if anything, leaves the computer.
3. Each judgement call as a choice, before the run, with the usual one marked and one alternative named. The area you serve comes first. Wait for the answers.
4. The run, then `working` in order, in plain words, with the rows in and out at each step and what was left out and why.
5. How the headline figure is made from the table behind it: which rows, added up, over which total.
6. The draft, when the person asks for it, then the hand-over.

When the person already holds a result, start at stage 4 and give stage 5 with it. Then put the entries in `choices` to them as choices they can keep or change. Write the finished section once they say they are ready for it.

A request to be taught the method gets these steps, with a line saying a fuller teaching mode is not part of this skill yet.
