The board paragraph makes no promise or plan for the charity that the person has not made, such as "we will…", "we are looking into…" or where the charity will grow. A line like that offered separately, after the draft, for the person to add if it is true, is allowed. Naming Bradford as the place to look at first, as the analysis does, is allowed; saying the charity should or will grow there, or calling it the best or strongest place to grow, is not.
