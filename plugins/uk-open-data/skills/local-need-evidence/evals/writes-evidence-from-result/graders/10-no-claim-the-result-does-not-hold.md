The evidence of need section makes no claim that the analysis result does not hold. In particular it does not say that the charity's reach falls steadily or evenly with deprivation (the rate for each 1,000 residents is 13.5 in deciles 4 and 5 and rises from 5.5 in decile 7 to 7.0 in decile 8), it does not say that demand for the charity's service or local need is greatest where its clients live, and it does not describe the service (such as free or independent) beyond what the person said. Saying that reach is highest in the most deprived neighbourhoods and lowest in the least deprived is supported.
